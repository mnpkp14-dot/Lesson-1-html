My favorite Receip

Fried rice

Fried rice is my favorite food because it is delicious and easy to cook

It is made with rice, eggs, chicken, vegetables, and soy sauce.

I like eating fried rice with cucumber and a fried egg. It is a perfect meal for lunch or dinner.

Ingredient

  • cooking oil
  • eggs
  • cooked rice
  • Garlic
  • Soy sauce

Cooking steps

  1. Heat the oil in a pan
  2. Cook the garlic until fragrant.
  3. Add the chicken and cook well.
  4. Add the eggs and stir.
  5. Add the rice and soy sauce.
  6. Mix everything together and serve.
